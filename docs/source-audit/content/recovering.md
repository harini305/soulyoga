Poor posture, injury, overuse and other factors can cause problems such as muscle pain, decreased mobility and postural imbalances. These problems can be addressed through techniques such as pressure, stroking and stretching using RecoveRing. In particular, Recovering’s two edges are designed with an optimal structure for fascia release, allowing for stroking along the muscle fibres to release tension in the fascia.
